Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the difference between Cumulative and Monthly Payout FD?

In a Cumulative Fixed Deposit, interest is compounded quarterly and paid out together with your initial principal at maturity. In a non-cumulative Monthly or Quarterly payout FD, interest is calculated and transferred directly to your bank account at regular intervals, providing a stable passive income stream.

How does Step-Up SIP increase long-term wealth?

A Step-Up SIP automatically increases your monthly investment contribution by a fixed percentage (e.g., 10% every year) as your income grows. Over 10-20 years, this small annual bump significantly increases compound capital growth compared to a regular flat SIP.

How is reducing balance Loan EMI computed?

Loan EMIs use the standard reducing balance formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N]/[(1+R)^N-1]. With each payment, the interest portion decreases while the principal repayment portion increases over time.
