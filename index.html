Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How is Fixed Deposit (FD) interest calculated for different payout frequencies? ▼

For cumulative deposits, interest compounds every quarter (4 times a year) using the compound interest formula: A = P(1 + r/4)^(4t). For monthly, quarterly, or yearly non-cumulative payouts, interest is calculated and paid out as regular income without compounding.

How much money can I save by making part-prepayments on a loan? ▼

Making even small extra monthly prepayments reduces your remaining principal balance directly. Because monthly interest is charged on the remaining balance, prepayments shorten your loan tenure and can save thousands in total interest payments over time.

What is a Step-Up SIP and why is it better than a normal SIP? ▼

A Step-Up SIP automatically increases your monthly investment amount by a chosen percentage every year (e.g. +10%). This aligns with yearly salary increments and significantly boosts final investment returns compared to keeping your SIP amount fixed.
